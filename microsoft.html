What Microsoft is

For small and mid-sized organisations, Microsoft offers Dynamics 365 Business Central. It is aimed mainly at wholesale and professional services firms, and also handles assembly, manufacturing, service management and warehouse operations. Larger organisations can step up to Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain.

Its main strength is how it connects to the rest of the Microsoft stack. Records can be shared in Teams, customer data shows in Outlook, data moves in and out of Excel, and Power BI reports are built in. Power Automate and Power Apps extend it with workflows and small apps. It is sold as a per-user subscription and set up through a Microsoft partner.
