    Institutions we follow
      Where the tax rules come fromPwC Tax Summaries (reviewed 22 October 2026), the RSM Uganda Tax Guide 2026/27 and a PAYE summary updated for the 1 July 2026 changes. URA’s own older pages may still show the previous bands.
      About the rates and headlinesRates are loaded each time you open the page and refreshed every few minutes from ExchangeRate-API, with a backup from the currency-api project. They are mid-market reference rates, not live dealing rates, so a bank or money changer will quote differently. Insights are calculated from those rates and their history. Headlines come from news publishers on our trusted list and link to the publisher, and the official sources are linked directly. The financial insights centre collects posts from the institutions’ own websites and links back to each one. If a feed cannot be reached, the page says so instead of showing old figures as current.
      Estimates, not adviceThese tools give estimates for planning. They are not tax, legal or investment advice. Confirm figures with URA, or talk to us before you file.
